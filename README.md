# Who Wins What in DoD Contracts

Brody Kretz

Across Department of Defense contract awards from 2020 through 2026, how do the biggest defense contractors differ in what they get paid for, and how dependent is each one on a single category of work or a single branch of the military? This project builds a profile for each of nine companies — the five biggest primes (Lockheed Martin, RTX, Boeing, General Dynamics, Northrop Grumman) and four newer defense tech companies (Palantir, Anduril, Shield AI, Scale AI) — covering their share of DoD dollars by product/service category, by military branch, and by whether the work was competed or sole source. Data comes from the USAspending API, with a scrape of defense.gov's daily contract announcements as a cross-check.

## Data

Pulled from the [USAspending API](https://api.usaspending.gov) (`spending_by_award` and `spending_by_category` endpoints) for DoD prime contract awards, 2020–2026, plus a sample of [defense.gov contract announcements](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/) as a secondary check.
